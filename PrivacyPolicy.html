1. Who we are

Nur Al-Quran (“the App”) is an independent app for reading the Qur'an, listening to recitation, following prayer times, the Qibla, and daily adhkar. It is published for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Questions about privacy, or about anything in this policy: mohammad.karim94@gmail.com.

2. Your location

The App asks for location permission so that it can work out prayer times for where you actually are, schedule the adhan notifications, keep the home-screen and lock-screen widgets correct, and point the Qibla compass towards the Kaaba.

On a first install, the App explains this on its last onboarding screen before the system permission dialog appears, and asks only when you tap “Enable”. If you choose “Not now”, or skip onboarding, nothing is requested. You can grant or revoke location at any time in your device settings, and you can use the Qur'an, tafsir, recitation and adhkar without it.

Where your coordinates go

Prayer times are calculated from your coordinates. The App does this by asking public prayer-time services, which means your latitude and longitude are sent to those services. We want to be exact about which ones:

Service What it receives Why
api.aladhan.com Your latitude and longitude rounded to about 1.1 km, the date, your calculation method, your Asr school, your time zone name, and any minute adjustments you set To return the prayer timings for that place and day
api-bdc.net (BigDataCloud) Your latitude and longitude rounded to about 1.1 km, and the language you have chosen in the App (ar or en) Reverse geocoding: to turn them into a city name, so the App can show “your location” as a place rather than numbers, and in your own language. Only the city-level answer is kept
mpt-server.vercel.app Your latitude and longitude rounded to about 1.1 km Only if you use the Malaysia calculation method — to find which JAKIM zone you are in

These requests carry no name, no account and no identifier — nothing that ties the coordinates to you or to your other requests. Like any web request, they do reveal your IP address to the service you are contacting. Each service handles what it receives under its own privacy policy, and we have no access to their records.

If you select one of the official national providers, it is given less: www.e-solat.gov.my receives a zone code, data.gov.sg receives a date, and portal.moi.gov.qa receives nothing at all.

Your last known coordinates and city label are also stored on your device, so prayer times and widgets work without a lookup every time — including inside the iOS App Group and Android shared preferences that the widgets read from. The app sends coordinates to the external services listed above when it needs their response. If you separately enable background Prayer Live Activity updates, the configured service gets only the city label, time zone and localized prayer schedule, never latitude or longitude.

3. Your account, and what syncs

The App can be used from start to finish without an account. Nothing is behind a sign-in: the Qur'an, tafsir, recitation, prayer times, the Qibla, adhkar, your khatma and your prayer record all work on a device that has never signed in. On first launch the App creates an anonymous account for itself so that your record has somewhere to live; it carries no name, no email and nothing that identifies you.

You may sign in with Apple or Google, and the only reason to is this: an anonymous record lives on one device, and signing in lets it follow you to a new phone. When you do, the anonymous record you already have is joined to the account rather than replaced.

What the account holds

Data Where it comes from, and why
Email address From Apple or Google when you sign in, so the same account can be recognised on another device. If you use Apple's “Hide My Email”, we receive only the relay address and never your real one.
Name Only if Apple or Google passes one at sign-in. It is never required, and nothing in the App displays it to anybody else.
User ID The account identifier every synced row carries. It is how your record is told apart from somebody else's, and nothing more.
Your own content Prayer and fasting records, reading progress and the last page you were on, hifz plans and reviews, khatma membership and which juz you took, and the text of any reflections you write at an ayah.
Khatma display name Only if you join a group khatma. You choose it yourself when you join, it can be anything, and it is shown to the other members of that group — with which juz you took, and nothing else.

What other people in a group khatma can see

The display name you chose for that group, and which juz you have taken and read. Not your prayers, your fasting, your reading elsewhere in the Qur'an, your hifz, your reflections, your email or your real name. There is nowhere in the group for any of it to appear.

Where it is stored

On Supabase, in the European Union, which encrypts stored data at rest and data in transit. It is used to hold your record and for nothing else: it is not profiled, not analysed, not sold, and not shared with anyone. There is no advertising and no tracking anywhere in the App.

Deleting it

Settings → Account → “Delete account and all data”. One confirmation, and it deletes the account, every row belonging to it on the server, and the copy on your device. There is no form to fill in, no email to send and no waiting period. If you were in a group khatma, your membership and your display name go with it.

Signing out, by contrast, leaves your record on the server so that signing back in returns it.

4. Qur'an text, tafsir and recitation

Qur'an text, translations, tafsir and recitation are loaded from public Islamic content services. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content — a surah number, an ayah number, a reciter or tafsir ID, or a search term you typed. They carry no location and no identifier.

  • api.alquran.cloud and alquran.api.islamic.network — Qur'an text, translations and search
  • cdn.jsdelivr.net — tafsir text
  • api.quran.com — the reciter catalogue
  • api.quranpedia.net — ayah study material
  • download.quranicaudio.com, mp3quran.net, media.way2quran.com — recitation audio

As with any media server, the audio hosts can see which file you are streaming, which means which surah and reciter. Nothing accompanies that beyond your IP address.

5. What stays on your device

Whether or not you sign in, the App keeps a working copy of everything on your device, and it is what the App reads from. What a signed-in account adds is a second copy on the server, described in section 3 — the two are kept in step. The settings and caches below never leave the device at all. The optional background Prayer Live Activity feature has a separate, limited data flow described below.

Category Examples
Settings Language, reciter, tafsir edition, calculation method, Asr school, adhan and iqama adjustments
Location Last known coordinates and city label
Reading Bookmarks, last read position, search history
Adhkar and tasbih Your counters, daily log and streak
Activity App-open counts and active days, shown on your own profile screen
Caches and downloads Qur'an text, tafsir and any recitation you download for offline use

“Clear cache” in Settings removes the cached text and downloads. Uninstalling the App removes everything. If your phone's own backup includes app data, this data may be included in your backup, held by Apple or Google under their terms — not by us.

6. Crash reports and diagnostics

None are collected. If the App hits an error it recovers on your device and writes the detail to the local device log. There is no crash-reporting service in the App — no Crashlytics, no Sentry, nothing — and no diagnostic data is transmitted.

Apple and Google may still collect their own crash data at the operating-system level if you have agreed to share it with them. That is between you and them, and we cannot see it.

7. Notifications and alarms

Notification permission is used to tell you when a prayer time arrives and when the morning and evening adhkar windows open. Notifications are scheduled on your device. The optional Prayer Live Activity setting uses ActivityKit pushes only to refresh its Lock Screen presentation; it does not send adhan alerts.

Optional background Prayer Live Activity updates

In a build configured with the service, you can enable this in Prayer & Adhkar reminder settings. It is off until you turn it on. The service receives a random installation ID, an authentication secret (stored only as a one-way hash, so the stored copy cannot be used to impersonate you), ActivityKit push tokens, your city-level label, time zone, app language and a two-day localized prayer schedule. Your coordinates are never sent to it, and it holds nothing else — no name, no account, no advertising identifier, no reading history. It is hosted in the European Union (Ireland), on Supabase infrastructure that encrypts stored data at rest; from there it reaches Apple's push servers and nowhere else. It deletes the record when you turn the setting off, and a record not heard from for 48 hours is deleted automatically, because the schedule it holds covers two days and has by then run out. If you are offline when you turn it off, deletion is retried while the app can reach the service and on the next launch.

What it does with all of that is narrow: at each prayer event in the schedule you uploaded it sends the same schedule back to your phone, because an ActivityKit push is what makes iOS redraw the Lock Screen banner. It performs no prayer-time calculation of its own and sends you no alerts.

iOS ends a Live Activity roughly eight hours after it starts, so the service hands over to a fresh one at 7 hours 45 minutes and can do so a few times per uploaded schedule. Open the app at least every two days to refresh that schedule; once it runs out, the banner says so and nothing further is sent. APNs delivery, network access, ActivityKit permissions and Apple's update limits can all affect delivery.

Android: “Alarms & reminders”

On Android 12 and later the App uses the SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permission so the adhan arrives at the right minute rather than whenever the system next gets around to it. If you turn it off, the App keeps working and the adhan still arrives — just not exactly on time. The App explains this on its own screen, in Settings → “Adhan on time”. The App does not use USE_EXACT_ALARM, the permission that cannot be revoked.

8. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It has no advertising, no analytics, no chat and no public profile, and nothing in it is recommended or targeted on the basis of who anyone is.

It does have an optional account (section 3) and one shared feature: a group khatma, which is joined by a six-character code given out by whoever started it. Inside a group a member sees only a chosen display name and which juz each person took — there is no messaging, no photographs and no free text shared between members. A parent or guardian who would rather none of that applied can simply not sign in; every other part of the App works the same way without it.

The optional background Live Activity feature may transmit a city-level location, prayer schedule and installation identifier when enabled. A parent or guardian can leave it off or turn it off at any time. If you have a concern about a child's data, please write to us.

9. Your rights

If you have an account, you can delete it and everything in it from inside the App — Settings → Account → “Delete account and all data”. It is immediate and it is complete: the account, the server rows and the local copy. You do not need to ask us, and there is nothing we keep back.

If you enable background Prayer Live Activity updates, you can remove the service's installation record by turning that setting off while online. The service retains no name or account profile. Its encrypted schedule and tokens are deleted on opt-out or after 48 hours without an app or token update. Other data described in section 5 stays on your device; clear it in Settings or uninstall the App.

For the coordinates sent to the prayer-time services in section 2, any rights you wish to exercise would be against those services directly, under their own policies. We have nothing to hand over or delete on their behalf.

10. Changes to this policy

If the App's handling of data changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated. A change that affects what leaves your device will also be described in the App's store release notes.
